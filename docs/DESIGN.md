# Design

The passes hang from pegs on a continuous metal rail under a soft light. Each stop centres one pass, with its neighbours turned slightly towards it. The wall picks up a tint from the artwork. On wide screens the title occupies the empty space at the start of the rail and moves into the header at the second stop.

The caption pairs the inscription with the event. A strip of fabric samples follows the same order as the rail. Drag across the samples to browse by colour or print. With the strip focused, arrow keys move one pass and Home or End moves to either end. Scrolling, Previous, Next and the Object index all select the same stop. The step buttons have a quiet clip sound with a mute control.

Selecting a hanging object or choosing **Pick up pass** opens a close-up. Its record gives materials, dimensions and available event details. On narrow screens the record follows the viewer. Escape or **Back to exhibition** returns to the same stop and restores focus.

Drag the badge to move it and release it to watch it settle. Shift-drag with a mouse to rotate and snap to the nearer face. Flip badge and the Front and Back controls work with touch and keyboard. **Read print** lays the selected face flat at a larger scale. Visitors can pan or scroll over the artwork, flip it or return to **Whole pass**. Only the focused badge hit target captures drag gestures, so the rest of the panel scrolls normally.

Reduced motion uses immediate stop and face changes, removes camera parallax and idle sway, and damps the focused badge more quickly. The static viewer preserves all twenty-two stops, both faces and their records when WebGL is unavailable.
