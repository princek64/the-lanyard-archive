# Design

The exhibition opens with editorial typography and a short introduction. Three suspended badges sit along a path through a charcoal scene. Each has its own spotlight. The camera moves between stops as the visitor scrolls. On desktop, neighbouring badges can appear in the distance. On narrow screens, the featured object has the available space.

Visitors can use the numbered stops or Previous and Next. Clicking a badge or choosing Inspect badge opens the object viewer and event record. Escape or Back to exhibition restores the current stop and returns focus to the opening control. On mobile, the record follows the viewer in the same scrollable panel.

Drag the badge to move it and release it to watch it settle. Shift-drag with a mouse to rotate and snap to the nearer face. Flip badge and the Front and Back controls also work with touch and keyboard. Only the focused badge hit target captures gestures, so the rest of the panel scrolls normally.

With reduced motion enabled, the exhibition camera and face changes are immediate, idle sway stops, release momentum is suppressed and the focused badge damps more quickly. The static viewer preserves the three stops, both faces and their records when WebGL is unavailable.
