# Design

The exhibition plays out on a cream page framed by yellow graph paper. Each stop pairs the actual pass with its inscription and event name. An original illustrated clasp guides the visitor across a cobalt London skyline. Bricolage Grotesque sets the event lettering and STIX Two Text sets the inscription.

The opening view shows the featured pass at its resting angle and final size from the first paint. Native scrolling moves the skyline and passes at different speeds, with a small step from the guide as the fabric swings. The camera, swing and step settle when scrolling stops. Reduced motion presents the settled framing immediately. On phones the caption moves above the scene and the entire loop of the lanyard fits on the page.

Previous, Next, the ribbon strip and Object index share the same stop. The index is a contact sheet of the badge fronts. Sound starts off. Turning it on previews a clasp snap, which also plays when the featured badge changes or a visitor picks up or returns a pass. Inspecting a pass adds a Viewed stamp for this visit and the guide takes out a magnifying glass. The inspection state lasts until the page is reloaded.

Selecting a hanging object or choosing **Pick up pass** opens a close-up. Its record gives materials, dimensions and available event details. On narrow screens the record follows the viewer. Escape or **Back to exhibition** returns to the same stop and restores focus.

Drag the badge to move it and release it to watch it settle. Shift-drag with a mouse to rotate and snap to the nearer face. Flip badge and the Front and Back controls work with touch and keyboard. **Read print** lays the selected face flat at a larger scale. Visitors can pan or scroll over the artwork, flip it or return to **Whole pass**. Only the focused badge hit target captures drag gestures, so the rest of the panel scrolls normally.

The archive opens with server-rendered images. Picking up a pass loads the live 3D viewer. Its static fallback preserves both faces, the enlarged print and the event record.

The Solana pass has a **Use badge** control beneath the viewer. Its LCD and indicator lights run Solana OS. Tap the six buttons on the case or use the arrow keys, Enter and Escape. Home returns to the firmware launcher. **Put down** resumes the hanging view and keeps the current app available until the visitor returns to the wall.
